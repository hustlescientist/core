# CORE project brief

Baseline: October 7, 2026. This is a working implementation summary, not an approved final site or a complete content export.

## Selected architecture

The public website is a custom HTML/CSS/JavaScript SPA embedded in the GoHighLevel website editor. Member-facing functionality uses GHL's native membership/client portal through the CRM, not a separately built authentication application.

The website introduces and connects; the portal supports participation; the CRM manages the relationship. Keep program information, essential resources, donation access, and initial volunteer information public.

## Intended public experience

Navigation: Programs; Students & Families; Schools & Partners; Get Involved; Resources; About & Impact. Persistent utilities: English / Espanol, Member Login, Donate.

Build audience pathways, the three program pillars, practical family/partner information, a searchable resource directory, public events/opportunities, support pathways, approved staff/board and impact content, stories, FAQs, and staff-owned inquiry forms. These are planned features, not all implemented in the scaffold.

## Intended native member experience

Audience groups: parents/guardians, scholars/students, teachers/school partners, volunteers/mentors, donors/sponsors, community/employer partners, and alumni. Use approved native courses, offers, groups, and contact-specific document sharing as appropriate. Staff work in GHL with staff permissions.

A requested role is not authorization. Verify the person and approval before granting access. CRM tags describe/process membership; actual course/group/document permissions enforce access. Direct student participation and community messaging require separate safeguarding approval.

Do not promise custom household switching, live student case-record dashboards, automatic school-wide access, unrestricted adult-to-minor messaging, or a complete donation history from unrelated payment processors. Confirm Salesforce's existing reporting role before planning a migration.

## Delivery order

1. Approve source content/brand/media; complete the public SPA and bilingual inquiry journeys.
2. Configure and test native GHL portal content/access and CRM follow-up.
3. Finish production routing, SEO, whole-task accessibility, real-device QA, and controlled publishing.

Success is task completion: families find help, inquiries reach staff, supporters can give, and approved members can use native services without exposing private information. No performance or conversion improvement is promised by the scaffold.
